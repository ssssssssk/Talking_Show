The Talking Show is a radio programme dedicated to talking in/as/for visual art that has aired every Wednesday night at midnight on Los Angeles' KCHUNG Radio since the station's inception in 2011. The show's hosts have always been Nicolas G. Miller and Steve Kado. Note: our archives have been taken off-line due to a mis-hap at KCHUNG. We are working very slowly to restore service, and update our archive to account for at least some of 2024.
